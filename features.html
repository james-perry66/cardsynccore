AI card recognition

Scan a stack in minutes, not hours

Our own recognition engine matches each scan against the full catalogue and reads the collector number. It shows its best match plus two alternatives: press Enter to accept, or 1 / 2 to pick another. Cards it isn't sure about get a second look from a stronger AI model.

  • Vision model matching against every catalogue image
  • Collector number read from the card, to tell reprints apart
  • Holo and reverse holo detected from the photo
  • Everyday scans run on our own engine, at no per-scan cost
  • High Accuracy: a stronger AI model re-reads hard cards
  • Pokémon today; Magic: The Gathering and Lorcana next
Inventory

Your inventory is the source of truth

Every marketplace reads from CardSync Core and reports back to it. Sell a card at a show and it comes off your online listings; sell it online and your counter stock is right.

  • Shared card catalogue with images, market prices and graded prices
  • Shop inventory by condition, with photos for listings
  • Personal collection kept apart from shop stock, with set and total value
  • Sealed product alongside singles
  • Weighted-average cost, so profit on every sale is real
  • Barcode labels with your own prefix
Buying

Buy collections with confidence

Price a collection at current market before you make an offer, then record the purchase so every card carries its share of what you paid.

  • Quotes you can print or send
  • Bulk purchases with cost spread across the cards
  • Grading candidates: raw cards worth sending to grade
  • Bargains: cards listed well under market price
