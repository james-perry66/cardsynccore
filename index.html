How a scan becomes a priced card

Recognition runs on our own models, so everyday scans cost nothing per card. A stronger AI model is there for the hard ones.

Recognise

A vision model compares the scan with every catalogue image and finds the closest matches.

Read

Text recognition reads the collector number, which tells apart reprints with identical artwork.

Finish

A model trained on our own scans tells normal, holo and reverse holo apart from the photo.

Second look

When the engine isn't sure, a stronger AI model re-reads the card. You confirm with one key.
